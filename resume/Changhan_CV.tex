% ======================= Research Interest ============================
\section{Research Interest}
Over my PhD years, I hope to infer the minimal structure of quantum gravity solely from general relativity
and quantum mechanics. No string theory or loop quantum gravity. I successfully derive holographic entropy
formulas and construct gauge-invariant observables in, for instance, slow-roll inflation, two-dimensional
dilaton gravity, and general regions bounded by extremal surfaces. This opens up a promising research
program for von Neumann algebras as the bottom-up framework for quantum gravity.

\vspace{4pt}
I also study data-efficient training of deep neural networks and the science of reinforcement learning.
My empirical work has explored accelerating diffusion language model pretraining and using agents to
improve MoE architectures. More recently, I focus on developing a principled framework for reinforcement
learning in large language models, in which scores and critics form coupled martingales, with applications
to variance reduction.

% ======================= Selected Projects ============================
\section{Selected Projects}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \entry{The two principles of LLM policy gradients \normalfont(work in progress)}{Oct. 2026}\\
        Developing a framework for LLM policy gradients using the martingale structure of cumulative scores
        and conditional expected rewards; exploring branching and control variates for variance reduction.
  \item \entry{\href{https://github.com/Chang-Han-Chen/autoresearch-moe}{\textcolor{black}{MoE autoresearch}}}{Jun. 2026}\\
        Explored LLM-agent-driven ``autoresearch'' on autoregressive mixture-of-experts transformers,
        stacking interventions to a 1.75\% bits-per-byte gain that persists under compute-optimal scaling.
  \item \entry{\href{https://github.com/Chang-Han-Chen/sample-efficient-dlm}{\textcolor{black}{Optimal block-size curriculum for diffusion language models}}}{Jun. 2026}\\
        Designed a block-size curriculum for pretraining block-diffusion language models, sweeping the
        autoregressive vs.\ diffusion training budget and finding a consistent sample-efficiency optimum near 30\%.
  \item \entry{\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01706}{\textcolor{black}{Holographic algebras at null infinity}}}{Sep. 2026}\\
        Constructed algebras at null infinity that reconstruct bulk regions in asymptotically flat spacetimes;
        recovered generalized black hole entropy from a regulated von Neumann algebra.
  \item \entry{\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.04311}{\textcolor{black}{An apologia for islands}}}{Jun. 2025}\\
        Showed how entanglement islands and the Page curve arise with massless gravitons and no external
        reservoir; constructed compactly supported gravitational observables to all perturbative orders.
  \item \entry{\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02116}{\textcolor{black}{A clock is just a way to tell the time}}\ \normalfont(with G.\ Penington)}{Jun. 2024}\\
        First demonstration of gauge-invariant algebras without an observer, contrary to the common belief.
        Derive holographic entropy for cosmological spacetimes and more general subregions.
  \item \entry{\href{https://inspirehep.net/files/c1b52950194cd41895084217803489d7}{\textcolor{black}{Geometry and entanglement in AdS/CFT and beyond}}}{May 2022}\\
        Independent senior thesis, awarded MIT's Morse/Orloff prize for best senior thesis in theoretical physics.
\end{itemize}
